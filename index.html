FitLoop

Trade. Alter. Repeat. For Paws.

A circular pet-product initiative transforming discarded textiles into clean, sized essentials for dogs—keeping fabrics in motion and out of landfills.

Trade Your Textiles

The Problem We Solve

Instead of empty zero-waste claims, FitLoop takes direct action against textile waste. We collect clean, discarded clothing and fabric items from the community, altering them into long-lasting blankets, durable mats, and sturdy leashes built specifically for canine durability.

The FitLoop Cycle

1. Trade

Exchange or donate your clean, discarded clothing and fabrics rather than throwing them away.

2. Alter

We handcraft suitable materials into durable dog essentials sized for every breed.

3. Use

Your dogs get cozy, upcycled blankets, heavy-duty floor carpets, and braided walking leashes.

4. Repeat

Return damaged or worn goods to us for repair, repurposing, or proper textile fiber recovery.
